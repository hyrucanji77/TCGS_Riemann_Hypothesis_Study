The package additionally contains \path{scale_endpoint_diagnostics.py}, \path{shifted_tower_diagnostics.py}, \path{completed_tower_diagnostics.py}, \path{kinetic_rate_diagnostics.py}, and \path{sesquilinear_diagnostics.py}, with their machine-readable outputs. There are ten scientific diagnostic scripts in total, with two separate verification drivers. The driver \path{verify_release.py} reruns the nine scripts in its explicit manifest, including \path{boundary_trace_diagnostics.py} and \path{sesquilinear_diagnostics.py}, in 13 fresh-directory executions. It checks the regenerated JSON files against the packaged outputs and the printed values in Tables~\ref{tab:moments}--\ref{tab:determinants} and Equations~\eqref{eq:robinvalues}--\eqref{eq:robinsecondnumerical}; it does not invoke \path{screw_kernel_diagnostics.py}.

The script \path{sesquilinear_diagnostics.py} performs 35 exact Gaussian-rational checks of the conjugate-linear first-slot convention, conjugate symmetry, scalar and phase laws, real--imaginary decomposition, the trace--Gram identity, and polynomial-adjoint conjugation, including negative controls with the first-slot conjugation deliberately omitted. These manufactured checks verify the conventions in Sections~6.1, 8.1, and~12.1; they do not construct the arithmetic insertion map.
