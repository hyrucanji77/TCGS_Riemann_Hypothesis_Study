We develop moment, prime-trace, and continuous-kernel formulations of the Riemann hypothesis and establish spectral exclusions. For the normalized entire function $F(w)=\xi(\tfrac12+i\sqrt w)/\xi(\tfrac12)$, defined by its even power series, we prove the equivalence of RH, positivity of every reciprocal-square Hankel matrix generated by $-F'/F$, and a positive trace-class Fredholm determinant realization. The moment formulation is situated in classical Grommer--Hamburger theory, and an equivalent completed prime-trace identity is obtained in the absolutely convergent half-plane. The required counting law excludes ordinary compact elliptic spectra and regular boundary-power realizations. An exponential corridor matches both leading counting terms but has the wrong determinant; a controlled Whittaker expansion extends this exclusion to the full constant-coefficient Morse family with arbitrary scale, endpoint, constant shift, and fixed real separated boundary condition. Finite-to-infinite moment transfer and explicit trace and arithmetic error estimates delimit what numerical approximations establish. Exact Laplace and meromorphic identities connect the same target to Suzuki's screw kernel and nonlocal characteristic ratio, retaining zero multiplicity and distinguishing the relevant convergence topologies. Within TCGS--SEQUENTION, conditional Gram-transfer statements specify the identities an independently defined source/readout realization must satisfy. The remaining step is a source-derived positive representation satisfying the complete arithmetic identity, or the required nonlocal analytic limit. The Riemann hypothesis is not proved.
